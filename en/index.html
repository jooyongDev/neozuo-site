How we work

We agree on scope, cost and timing after discussing your needs.

  1. Talk

    We learn about your goals and current situation, and identify what we need to review.

  2. Define scope and quote

    We agree on features, deliverables, cost, timing and how progress will be reviewed.

  3. Build and review

    We develop to the agreed scope and review the results together. Changes are discussed before implementation.

  4. Launch and handover

    After review, we deploy and hand over the work. Further support follows the agreed terms.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get in touch without a project brief?

Yes. Tell us what you want to build. We also offer planning services to help define the features and development priorities.

Can you work with an existing codebase?

Yes. We review the existing code and product before agreeing on the changes and approach.

How do you determine scope and pricing?

We discuss your goals, required features, existing materials, budget and target timeline. If planning or a code review is needed first, we agree on its scope and cost separately.

How will I follow progress?

Before development starts, we agree on how often to share updates, which tools to use and how you can review the work.

What if requirements change?

We first check whether the request falls within the agreed scope. If it requires extra work, we discuss the impact on cost and schedule before proceeding.

What happens after launch?

Bug fixes, ongoing operations and new features are discussed separately. Support scope, duration and the process for requests are agreed in the contract.
