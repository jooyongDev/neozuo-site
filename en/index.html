How we work

After an initial discussion, we agree on scope, cost and schedule.

See frequently asked questions

  1. Discuss

    We learn about your goals and current situation and explain what we need to review.

  2. Agree scope & quote

    Together, we agree on features, deliverables, cost, timing and how to share and review progress.

  3. Build & review

    We build to the agreed scope and review the results together. Change requests are discussed before implementation.

  4. Launch & handover

    Once approved, we deploy and hand over the work. Further support follows the agreed terms.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get in touch without a project brief?

Yes. Tell us what you want to build. We also offer planning to define the features and development priorities.

Can you work with an existing codebase?

Yes. We review the existing code and product, then discuss the changes and approach.

How do you determine scope and pricing?

We review your goals, features, materials, budget and target timeline. If planning or code review is needed first, we agree on its scope and cost separately.

How will I follow progress?

Before development begins, we agree on update frequency, tools and how you can review the work.

What if requirements change?

We check whether the request is within scope. If extra work is needed, we discuss its impact on cost and schedule before proceeding.

What support is available after launch?

We distinguish bug fixes, operations and new features. Support scope, duration and how requests are handled are agreed in the contract.
