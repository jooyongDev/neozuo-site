Turn recorded lessons into materials for review

Academies needed more than automated learning materials: students needed an app to watch lessons and ask questions, while teachers needed a web interface to follow progress. NEOZUO develops and operates the web and app experience, backend, video processing and chatbot.

What we built

  1. Teachers and administrators upload lesson videos for AI processing.
  2. The service generates subtitles, summaries, section-by-section content, quizzes and PDF notes.
  3. Students watch lessons and ask the AI chatbot questions about the material.
  4. Teachers can review students’ viewing progress and quiz results.

Launch and operation

The service has been operating since November 2025 and is used by paying academy customers. NEOZUO continues to handle bug fixes and improvements.
