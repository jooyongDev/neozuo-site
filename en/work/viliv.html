Turning recorded lessons
into student review

The service helps students review lessons uploaded by their academy. Beyond AI-generated learning materials, it needed a student app for lessons and questions and a teacher web interface for tracking progress.

NEOZUO developed the web and apps, backend, AI video processing and chatbot, and continues to operate and improve them after launch.

Connecting the learning journey
after a video is uploaded

  1. Generate materials from lesson videos

    Video processing · Content generation

    Academy owners, administrators and teachers upload videos; AI generates captions, summaries, section content, quizzes and PDF notes.

  2. Ask questions while learning

    Student app · AI chatbot

    Students watch lessons in the app and can ask an AI chatbot about the content, using the videos and related materials together.

  3. Track each student’s progress

    Teacher web

    Teachers review each student’s viewing progress and quiz results, connecting learning and academy management in one service.

A service used by paying customers,
continuously operated and improved

The service has operated since November 2025 and is used by paying academy customers. As development partner, we continue to fix bugs and improve features.
