Student app released and used by the academy

From exam preparation to reviewing mistakes

The academy needed to manage tests and homework while giving students a way to complete and review their work. We built the teacher web console, student mobile apps and backend as one connected service.

What we built

  1. Teachers upload HWPX exam files and prepare tests for their classes.
  2. Students take tests in the app, view their results and revisit incorrect answers.
  3. Teachers review each attempt, including answers, scores and student review notes.
  4. Sequences, vocabulary rotation and OMR workflows support repeated practice and paper-based exams.

Launch and operation

The student app has been released on the App Store and Google Play and is used by the academy’s students.
