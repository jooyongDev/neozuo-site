Where academies sell courses
and students come to learn

Academies sell recorded courses directly to middle and high school students. Instructors needed course registration, sales and settlement management; students needed to buy, study and submit homework in one place.

We built the instructor and student web and apps, backend, payment and settlement features, and AI lecture analysis integration.

Course registration, sales, learning
and settlements in one flow

  1. Register courses and prepare for sale

    Instructor web & app

    Instructors create courses, upload videos, submit curricula for review and set prices. AI captions, quizzes and chatbot features are integrated into registration.

  2. From purchase to learning and homework

    Student web & app · Web payments

    Students explore courses and pay on the web using Toss Payments. We implemented individual and subscription purchases, web and app learning, homework submission and reviews.

  3. Review sales and settlements

    Sales management · Settlements

    Instructors review sales and settlements. We implemented monthly settlements for academies selling courses.

A private web beta
is underway

The web service has been in private beta since September 2026. Instructor and student mobile apps are included in the scope and have not yet been released on the App Store or Google Play.
