Connect course sales and learning

Instructors needed to create courses and manage sales and settlements. Students needed to purchase courses, learn and submit homework in one place. We developed the web and apps, backend, payments and settlement, and AI lecture analysis integration.

What we built

  1. Instructors upload lessons, submit curricula for review and set prices.
  2. AI subtitles, quizzes and chatbot features are integrated into course registration.
  3. Students buy individual courses or subscriptions through Toss Payments on the web, then study and submit homework through web or app.
  4. Instructors can review sales and monthly settlements.

Launch and operation

The web service has been in private beta since September 2026. Instructor and student apps are part of the development scope and have not yet been released in app stores.
